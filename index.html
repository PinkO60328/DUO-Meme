The lore

They only had one phone.

Miso is an orange kitten who thinks every notification is about her. Kenji is a shiba who has opinions about everything and reads none of the replies. Somewhere along the way they ended up sharing a single silver foldable, and honestly it's been chaos ever since.

She wants cat videos. He wants charts. The phone folds in half, so technically they both win. That's the whole idea behind $DUO: two very different friends, one screen, and an unreasonable amount of loyalty to each other.
